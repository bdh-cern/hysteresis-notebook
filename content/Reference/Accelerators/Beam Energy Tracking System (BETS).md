

The BETS binds the deflection strength of the kicker magnets to the energy of the beam such that, if triggered, they will ramp to the right energy and thus kick the beam onto the correct dumping trajectory.

The BETS consists of several subsystems:

- Beam Energy Aquisition (BEA) modules
- Beam Energy Meters (BEMs)
- Beam Energy Interlock (MEI) module
- The State Control and Surveillance System (SCSS)
