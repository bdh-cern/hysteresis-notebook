
During LHC cycles, and only when the beam is injected, there is an obvious change in the B-train drift, leading to a discrepency on the order of about a few $\mu T$ (0.01G) some seconds after the injection. This must be because of some change in the noise profile caused by the beam injection process.

# Possibilities

[[Laslett Mechanism]] - I thought that this was a smoking gun, as it usually only happens when the LHC beam is injected, but by comparing corrected, beam-in cycles to exceptional corrected, no-beam cycles shows that there is still a drift difference.
