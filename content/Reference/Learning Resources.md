# Learning Resources

Lectures, papers and notes used for studying frequency map analysis, resonances and nonlinear dynamics.

# Introductory Lectures

- [W. Hillert, *Transverse Linear Beam Dynamics* (CAS Introductory Course)](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1356988/contributions/5713241/attachments/2932017/5149223/TLBD.pdf): linear optics, gradient-error tune shift, beta beating, optical resonances, stopbands and auto-locking. A longer local copy (145 slides) has these on slides 117 to 124.
- [B. J. Holzer, *Beam Optics and Lattice Design* (CAS)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.6514): Twiss parameters, FODO cells, where α = 0 and β is extremal, Liouville's theorem.
- [I. Martin, *Nonlinear Beam Dynamics*, Lecture 1](nonlinear_dynamics.pdf) ([online](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1101643/contributions/4635244/attachments/2396152/4097067/Lecture_NLBD_1.pdf)): sources of nonlinear fields, resonances and stopbands, why only low orders matter, frequency map analysis.

# Frequency Map Analysis

- [J. Laskar, *Frequency analysis for multi-dimensional systems. Global dynamics and diffusion*, Physica D 67 (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(93)90210-R): the founding paper. Defines the two chaos indicators (regularity of the map and diffusion in time) and uses the pendulum as the model of a resonance.
- [J. Laskar, *Frequency map analysis and quasiperiodic decompositions* (2003)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305364): how NAFF works and how precise it is.
- [J. Laskar and D. Robin, *Application of frequency map analysis to the ALS*, Particle Accelerators 54 (1996)](https://s3.cern.ch/inspire-prod-files-6/60c607d5106719ac193ce8fdd6a9ad09): first accelerator application. Defines diffusion through the frequency evaluated over time windows [τ, τ + T].
- [D. Robin et al., *Global dynamics of the Advanced Light Source revealed through experimental frequency map analysis*, PRL 85, 558 (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.85.558): the first experimental frequency map, made with pinger kicks and turn-by-turn BPM data.
- [Y. Papaphilippou, *Frequency and diffusion maps for the SNS ring*, PAC 2001](https://proceedings.jacow.org/p01/PAPERS/WOPB011.PDF): a short, clear worked example. Separates the frequency map from the diffusion map and colours by tune difference on a logarithmic scale.
- [J. Laskar, *Frequency map analysis and particle accelerators*, PAC 2003](https://www.cpt.univ-mrs.fr/~hscopp04/Abstracts/Laskar.pdf): review covering folded maps and torsion.

# Resonances and Islands

- [C. J. Gardner, *Resonance excitation in the AGS Booster due to closed orbit distortion in multipole magnets*, BNL AGS/AD Tech. Note 387 (1994)](https://www.bnl.gov/isd/documents/85603.pdf): which multipoles drive which resonances. Stopband width depends on amplitude from third order upwards.
- [K. Ruisard, *Design of a nonlinear quasi-integrable lattice for resonance suppression at UMER*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.10020), chapter 2: plain introduction to transverse resonances, islands and why higher orders grow more slowly.
- [E. C. Cortés García et al., *Resonant island trapping in a fourth-generation synchrotron light source*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.12469): third-order islands at ESRF EBS, the "devil's staircase" of locked tunes, and capture by radiation damping.
- [*Space-charge effects during half-integer resonance crossing in the CERN PSB*](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.18044): how amplitude-dependent detuning creates islands at the half-integer resonance.
- [*Rapid crossing of a resonance during tune modulation*, OSTI 1504886](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1504886): exponential growth inside a half-integer stopband, and lossless crossing through detuning.

# Slow Extraction

- [P. J. Bryant, *Resonant Extraction* (CAS Erice 2017)](resonant_extraction.pdf) ([slides](https://indico.cern.ch/event/451905/contributions/2159064/attachments/1413666/2163316/ResonantExtraction.pdf)): the Kobayashi Hamiltonian, the stable triangle, and particles locking onto the separatrices without any island.

# Hamiltonian Dynamics and Chaos (General)

- [N. Murray and M. Holman, *The role of chaotic resonances in the solar system*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111602): libration versus rotation, the separatrix as "the cradle of chaos", and resonance overlap.
- [A. Stroup, *The Dynamics of Pendula: An Introduction to Hamiltonian Systems and Chaos*](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/porterm/research/exp2.pdf): the pendulum phase portrait, libration and the separatrix.
